# Veda Companion: AI and privacy

The Veda Companion is an AI assistant built into VEDA Online. This page explains what it reads, what it keeps, who else is involved, and the choices you have. A short version is shown the first time you open the companion; this page is the full version.

!!! abstract "In short"
    - **It looks at your model only when you ask.** Nothing is read before your question.
    - **Only the model you have open:** your own, or one its owner has shared with you. Never your other models, or anyone else's model unless it's shared with you.
    - **Read-only.** The companion cannot change your model, its inputs or its results.
    - **Your conversations are saved on servers in the EU**, so you can come back to them, and we may use them to improve the companion's answers. You can turn saving off or delete your history at any time.
    - **Answers are written by Anthropic's AI in the US.** What's needed for an answer is sent there while it is written. Anthropic does not train its models on it and deletes it within 30 days.

## Who is responsible

The Veda Companion is provided by **KanORS-EMR** ("KANORS", "we").
For any question or request about this page or your data, write to **support@kanors.com**.

## It is AI

Answers are written by an AI model (Claude, made by Anthropic), using the TIMES and Veda documentation and, when you ask, data from your open model. AI can be wrong. Check important numbers against VEDA Online before relying on them.

## What the companion reads

**Documentation.** The TIMES and Veda documentation, KanORS reference notes and the Veda forum. Private documentation, such as a customer's own model documentation, is shown only to users that customer has authorised.

**Your model, only when you ask.** When your question is about your model, the companion reads what it needs from the model you have open in VEDA Online: its inputs, results, reports and solve status. That can be your own model or one its owner has shared with you; for a shared model, it reads what VEDA Online shows you for it. It reads nothing about your model before you ask, and nothing from your other models or any model you don't have open. If it cannot be sure that something belongs to your open model, it leaves it out and tells you.

**Read-only, always.** The companion's access to VEDA Online data is read-only. It cannot create, change or delete anything in VEDA Online.

**Your account.** When you use the companion, the panel passes your VEDA Online session to the companion's server, which uses it only to ask VEDA Online who you are. The session is not stored. VEDA Online then tells the companion your user name, user type, plan and the domain of your email address (for example `kanors.com`), and identifies your licence by a one-way code. Your password, full email address and licence key never reach the companion.

## What we keep

Everything the companion keeps is stored on servers in the EU, operated by KANORS.

| What | Why | How long |
|---|---|---|
| Your questions, the companion's answers, and the steps it took to answer, including excerpts of the model data it read | So you can come back to a conversation, to investigate problems, and to improve the companion's answers | Up to **365 days**, then the text is erased automatically. Sooner if you delete your history. Not stored at all if you turn saving off. |
| Your ratings (thumbs up or down) and any comment you add | To find good and bad answers and improve them | Comments are erased when you delete your history. |
| Usage records: date, user name, AI model used, the VEDA Online model that was open, cost | To apply monthly allowances and run the service | As long as needed for allowances. They contain no question or answer text. |
| Technical logs: user name, model name, timings and errors | To keep the service running and fix faults | 90 days |

**When saving is off**, we keep only the usage records and technical details (which steps ran, how long they took, errors), without your question, the answer or any model data.

**Improving the companion.** We may use saved conversations, especially answers you have rated, to improve the companion: for example, to find questions it answered badly and fix them. Only KANORS staff working on the companion can read saved conversations. We do not use them to train AI models. If you don't want your conversations used this way, turn saving off or delete your history.

## Anthropic, the AI provider

To write an answer, your question, the conversation so far, and the documentation and model data needed for the answer are sent to **Anthropic** in the United States, which runs the AI model as our service provider, under its commercial terms. Anthropic:

- does not use this data to train its models;
- deletes it within 30 days, unless it must keep it longer by law or for enforcing its usage policy.

See [how long Anthropic stores commercial API data](https://privacy.claude.com/en/articles/7996866-how-long-do-you-store-commercial-api-data).

## Your choices

- **Turn saving off.** In the companion's settings, choose not to save conversations. It applies from then on.
- **Delete your history.** In the companion's settings, erase the text of all your saved conversations and your rating comments. This cannot be undone. Usage records stay, because your monthly allowance is counted from them.
- **Don't use it.** The companion is optional. VEDA Online works the same without it.

## Your data on request

To get a copy of your saved conversations, or to have your data corrected or deleted, write to **support@kanors.com**.

## Security

All connections to the companion are encrypted (HTTPS). Its access to VEDA Online data is read-only and limited to what it needs. Saved conversations can be read only by KANORS staff working on the companion.

## Changes to this page

If we change how the companion uses your data, we will update this page and show the short version again in the companion the next time you open it.
